# Must-try cruise drinks, grouped by moment. Name|why
@must Pool deck & beach
Coconut Patrón Margarita|Your ship favorite. Ask for Patrón Silver, fresh lime and a toasted-coconut rim.
Piña Colada|The cruise original. Upgrade it: "with a dark-rum float."
Painkiller|Creamier and more grown-up than a colada, with nutmeg on top.
Miami Vice|Can't choose? Half piña colada, half strawberry daiquiri.
Lava Flow|Strawberry "lava" swirling up through a colada.
Bushwacker|A boozy chocolate-coconut milkshake from St. Thomas.
Rum Runner|A Florida Keys fruit bomb. Great frozen.
Mango Margarita|Frozen, with a Tajín rim.
Blue Hawaiian|Photogenic, creamy and coconutty.
Mojito|The best non-frozen pool drink. Ask for extra mint and crushed ice.
Caipirinha|Brazil's lime-and-cachaça smash.
@must Sunset & sail-away
Aperol Spritz|The 3-2-1 sunset ritual, perfect for Mediterranean sail-aways.
Hugo Spritz|Elderflower and mint. Lighter than Aperol.
Paloma|Tequila and grapefruit soda. Lighter than a margarita.
Spicy Margarita (Jalapeño)|For anyone who likes heat.
Moscow Mule|Ice-cold ginger refresher.
Dark 'n' Stormy|Bermuda's ginger beer and black rum.
French 75|Gin, lemon and bubbles: a celebration in a flute.
Limoncello Spritz|Amalfi sunshine.
Bellini|Peach and prosecco, from Harry's Bar in Venice.
@must Martini Bar
Espresso Martini|Dessert and a nap-preventer in one.
Lychee Martini|Floral, fruity and elegant.
Key Lime Pie Martini|Graham-cracker rim. Basically dessert.
Passion Fruit Martini|With a shot of bubbly on the side.
Cosmopolitan|A classic for a reason.
French Martini|Pineapple foam and raspberry.
Lemon Drop|Sweet-tart with a sugar rim.
Chocolate Martini|For the chocolate lovers.
@must After dinner
Carajillo|Licor 43 and espresso over ice. Trendy and delicious.
Mudslide|The Grand Cayman original, blended.
White Russian|Creamy coffee nightcap.
Grasshopper|Mint chocolate chip in a glass.
Brandy Alexander|Silky cognac and chocolate.
Limoncello|Ice-cold, Amalfi-style.
@must Zero-proof
Virgin Piña Colada|All the vacation, none of the rum.
Coconut No-Garita|Your favorite margarita, zero-proof.
Nojito|Fresh mint, lime and soda.
Crodino Spritz|Italy's zero-proof aperitivo.
